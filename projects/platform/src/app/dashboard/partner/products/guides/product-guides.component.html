Before you buy

Open a product to see what it is, who it is for and what is available. Stock labels tell you whether an item is ready to order.

  • Read the short summary and the full description.
  • Check the price and stock label before adding to cart.
  • Ask support if anything is unclear.

Pay and track

Product orders are paid from your MarketSpase wallet. No card details are collected at checkout.

  • Add products to your cart and review the total.
  • Confirm the order from your wallet balance.
  • Follow status changes in My orders.

Use and care

Follow the instructions on the pack. If you are unsure whether a product is right for you, ask before ordering.

  • Keep the pack and its instructions.
  • Stop and seek advice if you react badly.
  • Contact support about a damaged or wrong item.

Delivery, cancellations and refunds

How delivery worksexpand_more

Orders are processed after confirmation. Physical products are delivered to the address on your profile; digital products and guides appear in your account.

Can I cancel an order?expand_more

You can cancel while an order is still Pending. Once it is Fulfilled, contact support to discuss a return. Cancelled orders are refunded to your wallet in full.

What if something is wrong?expand_more

Open My orders, find the order and contact support with the order details. Keep the product and packaging until the issue is resolved.
